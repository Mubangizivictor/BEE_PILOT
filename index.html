Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my ride?
We accept manual payments via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and Bank transfers. After booking, you'll be redirected to a secure confirmation page with payment details, which are also sent to you via WhatsApp.
Is the fare really fixed?
Yes! The quote generated during booking is your final price. It includes fuel, driver fees, and waiting time. No surge pricing, even during peak hours or bad weather.
Can I book for someone else?
Absolutely. Simply enter the passenger's name and contact information in the booking notes, or inform our dispatch team when confirming your ride on WhatsApp.
