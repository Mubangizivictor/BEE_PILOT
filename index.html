Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my ride?

We accept manual payments via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and Bank transfers. You'll receive payment instructions after confirming your booking on WhatsApp.

Is the fare really fixed?

Yes! The quote you see when booking is the final price. No surge pricing, no hidden fees, and no surprises.

Can I book for someone else?

Absolutely. Just enter the passenger's details in the notes section or provide them when confirming via WhatsApp.
